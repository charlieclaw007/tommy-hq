## Description

Cookie Jar turns intention into a daily practice.

Choose one clear standard for each of four pillars: Diet, Gym, Phone, and
Sleep. At the end of the day, mark what you completed. Complete all four and
you earn one cookie.

The cookie is not food, punishment, or a measure of worth. It is a visible
record of a day when your actions matched your plan. Every mark becomes
evidence for the identity, "I am a person who keeps promises to myself."

WHY IT WORKS
Tracking makes a goal harder to ignore. What gets recorded can be reviewed,
and what gets reviewed can be adjusted. Cookie Jar uses a simple feedback
loop: choose a specific action, record whether it happened, see the result,
and adjust tomorrow's plan.

STREAKS WITHOUT SHAME
One missed day is not a reset. A single missed day between two cookie days
does not end your streak. Partial days are kept as evidence, not erased.
A missed day is information. Returning is the skill.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Four fixed pillars with your own wording for each rule
• A one-tap evening check-in, saved instantly
• A jar that fills with cookies as the evidence builds
• A two-week rhythm view, with corrections allowed for the last 14 days
• One daily reminder at the time you choose, skipped on days you've already earned a cookie
• Everything stays on your device. No account, no sign-in, no tracking.

The cookie counter is a motivation symbol, not a health claim.

Important: This guide is educational and is not medical, nutritional,
psychological, or fitness advice. It does not diagnose or treat any condition
and does not promise weight loss, health improvements, or mental-health
outcomes. If tracking increases guilt, anxiety, compulsive behavior, or
disordered eating, pause and seek qualified support.
