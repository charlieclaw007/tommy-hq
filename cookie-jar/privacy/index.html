Cookie Jar

Privacy policy

Cookie Jar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your daily check-ins, your rules, and your reminder settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local database, and never leave it. The app has no account system, no server, no analytics, and no third-party software development kits. The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. Deleting the app, or using "Reset all data" in Settings, removes everything the app has stored. Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or retain.
